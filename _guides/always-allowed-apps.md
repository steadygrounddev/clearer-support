# Always Allowed apps

Some apps you always need: phone, maps, a banking app, messages from family. Add them to **Always Allowed** and no group will ever pause or close them, whatever its rules say.

## Add apps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Always Allowed**.
2. Choose the apps and websites you want to keep available.

To take one off, choose **Remove from Always Allowed**.

## What it does

- Always Allowed beats every rule: [limit groups]({{ "/guides/limit-groups/" | relative_url }}) and [access groups]({{ "/guides/access-groups/" | relative_url }}).
- Your groups aren't changed. An Always Allowed app stays in its groups but is never paused. If you remove it from the list, its groups' rules apply to it again.
- The list is on this device only. It doesn't sync.

## Individual apps only

You can add individual apps and websites. A whole category can't be Always Allowed, and picking every app in a category counts as the whole category. If you choose a category, Clearer keeps your apps and leaves the category out. Choose some of its apps instead.

## Allowances

If an Always Allowed app is in a group as an individual app, its time doesn't count toward the group's allowance. If it sits inside one of the group's categories, its time still counts toward the allowance (iOS can't leave an app out of a category's usage), but it's still never paused. Clearer shows a note in the group when this applies.

## Streaks

Adding an app to Always Allowed while one of the group's rules is in effect loosens that group and breaks its [streak]({{ "/guides/streaks/" | relative_url }}). Clearer warns you first.

## Good to know

An app you uninstall can stay in the Always Allowed list for a while. That's iOS holding onto it, not Clearer.

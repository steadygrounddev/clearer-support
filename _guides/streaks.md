# Streaks

A streak counts the days in a row you stuck with a group's rules. Every group has its own streak, and there's an overall streak across all your groups.

## How a day counts

A day is added to a group's streak at midnight when:

- the group had a rule in effect at some point that day (for an access group, that means it was closed), and
- nothing broke the streak.

A day when no rule was in effect doesn't add to the streak, and doesn't break it. A new group starts at 0, and today never counts until it's over.

## What breaks a streak

Clearer doesn't judge your limits, it just records what happens. A streak breaks, and goes back to zero, when you override a rule that's in effect:

- using an **unpause**,
- **loosening** a group while one of its rules is in effect: for example raising an allowance or shortening a pause, removing an app, adding an app to Always Allowed, or deleting the group.

For access groups, loosening means adding an Hours window, a place or "anywhere except," or removing an app.

**Tightening** a group never breaks a streak. Changing a group while none of its rules is in effect doesn't either.

A day with a break doesn't count, even if the streak was already at 0.

## You're warned first, never blocked

Before an action that would break your streak, Clearer tells you, for example "Unpausing will break your 12 day streak." The choice is always yours.

## The overall streak

The overall streak counts days across all your groups. It grows when a group becomes active during the day, and any break in any group zeroes it right away. At midnight it grows by one only if the day was extended and not broken.

## Progress tab

The **Progress** tab shows your current and longest streaks, a calendar, a heat map, trends and week-by-week bars, for everything or one group. You can rearrange and resize the cards, hide some, and share the dashboard or a single card as an image. Streaks and progress are free for everyone.

## Good to know

Streaks run on trust beyond what's above. Clearer doesn't detect changes to your device's clock or time zone, revoking Screen Time access, or loosening a group while none of its rules is in effect.

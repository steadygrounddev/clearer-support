# Focus filters

You can have Clearer's groups respond to an iOS Focus. For example, pause your social apps whenever Work Focus is on, or open your work apps only during it. This works through a **Focus filter**, which you add in the iOS Settings app.

## Add the filter

1. Open **Settings**, then **Focus**.
2. Choose the Focus you want, or make a new one.
3. Tap **Add Filter**, then choose **Clearer**.
4. Choose your groups:
   - Under **Limit during this Focus**, choose the [limit groups]({{ "/guides/limit-groups/" | relative_url }}) to pause.
   - Under **Open during this Focus**, choose the [access groups]({{ "/guides/access-groups/" | relative_url }}) to open.
5. Save.

A **Clearer** filter can sit on as many Focuses as you like. In the app, the Focus screen for a group has an **Open Settings** button that takes you to the Settings app.

## What happens

- **Limit groups** chosen in the filter are **paused while that Focus is on**. In a group's Focus settings you can change this to allow some time instead of pausing.
- **Access groups** chosen in the filter **open while that Focus is on**, and are closed otherwise.

A limit group can have one saved Focus setting. An access group can have one Focus rule.

## Good to know

- Clearer can't see which Focus the filter is on, or list your Focus modes. It only learns when the filter turns on or off. The app says "a Focus filter you added" rather than naming the Focus.
- Editing the filter while its Focus is on takes effect right away.
- If you switch straight from one Focus to another that shares a group, the group carries on without a gap.
- A Focus turned on by a schedule works the same as one you turn on by hand.
- Focus updates come from iOS and can occasionally arrive late. Open Clearer to bring things up to date.

## Troubleshooting

If a group isn't reacting to a Focus, check that the filter is added to that Focus in iOS Settings, and that your group is selected under the right heading (limit or open). See [Help]({{ "/help/" | relative_url }}).
